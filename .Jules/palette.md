## 2025-05-15 - [Improving Search Interaction and Accessibility]
**Learning:** Adding a "Clear" button to search inputs and visible icons improves user confidence and interaction speed, especially on mobile where keyboard deletion can be slow. Accessibility labels on icon-only buttons are crucial for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always include a clear button for text inputs and ensure all icon-only interactions have proper `accessibilityLabel` and `accessibilityRole`.
